A laptop's lifespan is decided by its battery.

VibeBattery keeps yours healthy for longer. Features other apps charge for are all free here. Open source, MIT, no telemetry.

Download for MacView on GitHub

curl -fsSL https://github.com/y-minion/vibebattery/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

First thing: set the limit to 80%.

Beta 0.2.0, tested so far on one Mac (M4 Pro, macOS 27.0.1). The terminal install opens without a Gatekeeper prompt. If you download the zip in a browser, open the app once, then use System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. The app is signed with a free Apple Development certificate and is not notarized.

Features other apps charge for — all free here

  • Menu bar status icon and a customizable menu window
  • Charge limit 50 to 100%, weekday schedules, one-time full charge, heat hold
  • Power flow and the hardware battery percentage
  • Low Power Mode switch and MagSafe LED modes
  • Health history with measured facts: hours at high charge and heat, cycles, estimated health
  • Terminal sessions that keep the Mac awake while a coding agent works (vbctl run)
  • Shortcuts actions, notifications, English and Korean UI

Why a charge limit helps

Lithium-ion cells age faster at a high charge, and faster still when warm. Apple documents a Charge Limit for this reason (support.apple.com/102338); Battery University explains the stress curve (BU-808). VibeBattery promises no months or years. It shows what it measured on your Mac.

Safe by design

VibeBattery changes nothing while AlDente, batt, battery or Battery Toolkit is installed or running. The optional root helper accepts only fixed requests, writes one SMC key (the MagSafe LED) and runs no shell. The only network request is a daily version check, which you can turn off.
