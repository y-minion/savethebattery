A laptop's lifespan is decided by its battery.

Save the Battery keeps yours healthy for longer. Features other apps charge for are all free here. Open source, MIT, no telemetry.

InstallView on GitHub

First thing: set the limit to 80% (Apple silicon, macOS 26.4 or later). The app offers it on first launch.

Beta 0.2.0, tested so far on one Mac (M4 Pro, macOS 27.0.1).

Install

Recommended: one line in Terminal. No security warning, and the app opens by itself.

curl -fsSL https://github.com/y-minion/savethebattery/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

  1. Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal and press Return.
  2. Paste with ⌘ V.
  3. Press Return. Save the Battery installs into Applications and opens.

Or download the disk image. Download savethebattery.dmg, open it and drag Save the Battery to Applications. Open the app once; macOS says it cannot verify it (if no warning appears, go straight on). Then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You do this once: the app is signed with a free Apple Development certificate but not notarized, which needs a paid Apple program.

After that, new versions install from inside the app: Settings › Updates › Update now. No Terminal, no warning.

Features other apps charge for — all free here

  • Menu bar status icon and a customizable menu window
  • Charge limit 50 to 100%, weekday schedules, one-time full charge, heat hold
  • Power flow and the hardware battery percentage
  • Low Power Mode switch and MagSafe LED modes
  • Health history with measured facts: hours at high charge and heat, cycles, estimated health
  • Terminal sessions that keep the Mac awake while a coding agent works (stb run)
  • Shortcuts actions, notifications, English and Korean UI

Why a charge limit helps

Lithium-ion cells age faster at a high charge, and faster still when warm. Apple documents a Charge Limit for this reason (support.apple.com/102338); Battery University explains the stress curve (BU-808). Save the Battery promises no months or years. It shows what it measured on your Mac.

Safe by design

Save the Battery changes nothing while AlDente, batt, battery or Battery Toolkit is installed or running. The optional root helper accepts only fixed requests, writes one SMC key (the MagSafe LED) and runs no shell. The only network request is a daily version check, which you can turn off.
